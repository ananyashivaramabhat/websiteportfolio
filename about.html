About

I like the part where the board first powers on.

I'm an embedded firmware engineer finishing an M.S.E. in Electrical and Systems Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania. Most of my work lives between a schematic and a register map: drivers, bootloaders, firmware update paths, control loops and the bring-up that makes new hardware trustworthy.

This year I'm writing bare-metal STM32 firmware for silicon photonic engines at Xscape Photonics. Before that I worked on embedded Linux at Siemens, test automation at Nokia, and resonator characterization at IISc. I grew up in Bengaluru, India, and studied electronics at BMS College of Engineering there.
